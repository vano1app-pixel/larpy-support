What counts as progress?

Body records movement with a photo or qualifying workout. Work needs a link or screenshot of a result. Learnt saves your takeaway. A focus timer measures time; it does not prove that work was finished. Extra entries on one date do not multiply that date’s activity reward.

Focus and app blocking

A basic timer leaves other apps available. Protected focus requires Larpy Plus, Screen Time permission and your own selection of apps or websites. During a break, all selected distractions become available. Return to Larpy to end the break early, or use End focus to stop the session and unblock apps.

If protection is unavailable, check Screen Time permission and choose your apps again. Contact support if a session does not end correctly. You can also revoke Larpy’s Screen Time access in iPhone Settings.

Larpy Plus and the free trial

Larpy Plus is an optional weekly subscription for protected focus, timed breaks and detailed weekly reviews. The App Store paywall shows the available local price and any introductory trial before you subscribe. A one-month introductory trial is intended for eligible new subscribers; Apple determines eligibility. Subscriptions automatically renew until cancelled.

To manage or cancel, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Larpy. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting Larpy does not cancel a subscription.

Manage Apple subscriptions · Request help with an Apple charge or refund

Use Restore purchases in Larpy’s Settings or on the Plus screen with the Apple Account used to subscribe. Free logging, basic timers, saved activities and earned outfits remain available without Plus.

Your activities and privacy

Larpy has no app account or sign-in. Activity history is stored on your device, and the activity/photo folder is excluded from iCloud backup. Deleting the app removes that local history. The optional Mac companion shares daily usage totals through an iCloud Drive folder you select; it is not a backup of your activity history.

Friend comparisons use explicitly shared, dated snapshots. They are not live account connections or authenticated scores. See the privacy policy for permissions, storage and sharing details.
