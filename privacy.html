Larpy: Focus Companion

Your day stays yours.

Privacy policy · Effective 6 October 2026

Larpy is developed by Ayush Puri. It does not require an app account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or send your activity history to a Larpy server. Optional features and sharing are described below.

Data stored on your device

Larpy stores your optional name, chosen path, preferences, activity notes, links, selected photos, outfit choices, weekly reflections and focus sessions locally. The activity and screenshot folder is excluded from iCloud backup because it can contain workout information. Completed focus-session details are retained for approximately 32 days. Deleting the app removes its local data; there is no Larpy account recovery or cloud history service.

Photos, microphone and speech

Larpy reads photos you select or take for an activity. It does not upload those photos automatically. Voice input is optional, requires microphone and speech permission, uses on-device recognition where available and places the transcription in an editable draft. Audio is not saved by Larpy. You can type when local recognition is unavailable.

Apple Health

With permission, Larpy reads completed workouts and stores their identifiers, dates, durations and source locally to count activity days. It does not request heart rate or medical records, write workouts, use Health data for advertising, or sell Health data. Disconnecting Health stops new imports; existing saved receipts remain until removed. You can also revoke access in Apple’s Health settings.

Calendar

With permission, Larpy displays event titles and times on your device. Plans do not count as completed activity. Apple’s event editor lets you review a focus block before adding it to a calendar. Calendar events are not uploaded to a Larpy server. Revoke access or disconnect the calendar to stop future reading.

Screen Time and focus

After you authorize Screen Time, Apple represents your selected apps, categories and websites as private tokens. Larpy stores those tokens in a local App Group shared with its focus extensions. It uses them to shield distractions during a session you start. Larpy does not inspect messages, screen contents or browsing history.

Focus task names, break reasons and times are stored locally. The shield may display your chosen task, so avoid sensitive details if others can see your screen. A break temporarily allows all your selected distractions; its time is excluded from recorded focus. You can end focus or revoke Screen Time permission. Widgets may display recorded activity and timer totals on your Home or Lock Screen.

Optional Mac usage totals

The Mac companion can read local Codex and Claude Code usage counters and place daily totals in an iCloud Drive folder you choose. Larpy reads that folder only after you connect it. This feature does not export prompts, conversations, code or provider keys, and it is not a connection to your ChatGPT or Claude website account. You can disconnect the folder at any time. iCloud is provided by Apple under its privacy terms.

Follower evidence and sharing

Follower counts and screenshots are self-reported. Larpy does not request social passwords or authenticate your social accounts. Removing a tracker removes its current saved count and screenshot; earned outfits remain.

When you choose to share a card, invitation or progress snapshot, Apple presents a share sheet and you choose the destination. A snapshot can include your chosen name and the dated totals shown in its preview. Review before sharing. Files already sent to others are controlled by their recipients; deleting them inside Larpy cannot recall another person’s copy.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and any in-app purchases. Larpy uses verified StoreKit transaction information, such as product identifier and expiry or revocation status, to provide access. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide the developer with transaction and financial reports under its own terms.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address and message are used to answer your request and maintain necessary support records. Share only information needed to resolve the issue. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary connection logs under its privacy statement. This site adds no analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can decline optional permissions and use the basic app. Manage permissions in iOS Settings, disconnect integrations in Larpy’s Connections screen, and remove saved records using the app’s available controls. Contact the developer about access, correction or deletion of support correspondence. You may also contact your local data-protection authority, including Ireland’s Data Protection Commission.
